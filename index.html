Questions

Is it safe to connect my accounts?
Sunk only reads your purchase history — it can't spend, trade, or post anything on your behalf.
What platforms are supported?
Roblox and Steam at launch, with PlayStation, Xbox, and Epic support coming shortly after.
Is the leaderboard public by default?
No — it's off until you turn it on, and you choose exactly who sees it and what's shared.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, no contracts — cancel from Settings whenever you want.
